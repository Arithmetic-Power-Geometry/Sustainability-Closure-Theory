# Phase III — Strong-baseline tests

Local reproducibility tests were expanded from 7 to 9 passing tests.

## Rebound-aware energy vs full closure

A 41-member family held deployment-time operational intensities and demand elasticity fixed while varying only future replacement feedback. All 41 cases remained better than baseline under cumulative rebound-aware energy. Full SCT closure judged only 22/41 better; 19/41 became worse after future replacement burden was included. The first disagreement occurred at replacement-feedback coefficient 0.22.

## Dynamic carbon vs multi-resource closure

A 36-member family varied candidate water intensity while keeping a lower carbon intensity. Dynamic cumulative carbon ranked the candidate better, but full closure reversed in 14/36 cases. The first reversal occurred at water-intensity 1.35 in the current normalized model.

## Scientific interpretation

These results do not establish empirical environmental thresholds. They show a model-level distinction: even a rebound-aware energy comparator can miss non-energy future-state burdens, and a dynamic carbon comparator can miss burden migration.

## Current readiness decision

The next gate is the abstract theorem proof and a consequential-LCA-inspired comparator. The full paper should still not be frozen until those tests and at least one externally grounded data/trace experiment are complete.
