# Phase VI-D/E — Robustness and uncertainty attack

This phase stress-tests the externally grounded ordering result from Phase VI.

PUE and WUE in the six-project equal-`reported_size` witness are perturbed multiplicatively at 0%, 0.5%, 1%, 2%, and 5%. These are **stress-test sensitivity scales**, not empirical error bars. Each level uses 5,000 deterministic-seed trials.

The experiment measures persistence of at least one PUE/WUE ordering reversal, survival of the baseline reversal set, preservation of the baseline PUE-WUE Pareto frontier, and sensitivity of the preferred project to the declared PUE-versus-WUE scalarization weight.

A robust SCT result does not require a single winner to remain fixed. Weight-dependent winners show that scalar sustainability ranking requires an explicit value judgment. The structural target is persistence of observation-class sensitivity and cross-resource ordering conflict.
